## 6. Bitcoin sweep — how the money actually becomes BTC

You said "put it into bitcoin." Here's the disciplined version (this is the part that
protects you from yourself):

1. **Don't lump-sum the whole thing into BTC the day it lands.** $40k is a lot to dump at
   one price. **DCA: sweep a fixed % of every payment into BTC weekly.**
2. **Suggested split per dollar earned:**
   - **60% → BTC** (the goal) — bought every Friday, same time, regardless of price.
   - **25% → cash/tax reserve** — this income is taxable; set it aside *before* you feel rich.
   - **15% → reinvest** in the business (ads, a VA to run outreach, better tooling → more clients).
3. **Mechanics:** reputable exchange (Coinbase/Kraken/Strike) with **recurring buys** so it's
   automatic and emotionless. Move meaningful BTC to **self-custody** (hardware wallet) once
   it's sized up — not your problem in week 1, very much your problem at $20k+.
4. **Risk note (read it):** going hard into a single volatile asset can draw down 50%+. The
   25% reserve and 15% reinvest exist so a BTC crash doesn't take out your runway *or* your
   tax bill. Sustainable beats heroic.
