## What it is / what it does
- Self-hosted interface for talking to language models: chat, autonomous agents, tools, model serving, research, email, calendar, document editing, persistent memory.
- Supports any local model or API backend: **vLLM, llama.cpp, Ollama, OpenRouter, OpenAI**.
- **Cookbook** feature: scans your hardware, recommends models your GPU can actually run, then downloads them for you.
- **Agent** that browses the web, edits files, has shell access, transcribes video, and auto-handles email.
- **Self-evolving:** the agent crystallizes what works into its own instructions over time.
- License: open source, free. "No sales team, no demo request."

## The novel angle (feeds Why-this-one)
Not the features — the *distribution*. Self-hosted/local AI has always been gated by technical friction, and adoption stayed niche-developer. A creator with a hundred-million-person audience embedding local inference into entertainment-first content collapses that gate at a scale no developer-focused launch ever has. The story is "build-in-public at planetary reach": he spent the past year on camera ditching the cloud and learning Linux from scratch, then handed the result to everyone and asked for nothing.
