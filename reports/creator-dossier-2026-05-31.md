## RuView — what it is (verified against the repo)
Turns ordinary WiFi radio into spatial intelligence with no camera. Reads Channel State Information (CSI) off ESP32 sensors and converts the signal disturbances caused by movement and respiration into presence, motion, vital signs, and pose.

- Multi-band CSI fusion across **6 WiFi channels** via time-division multiplexing.
- **Spiking neural networks** that self-calibrate locally within **30 seconds**.
- Self-supervised contrastive learning (**12.2M training steps** on 60K frames).
- Presence and multi-person counting; breathing (6-30 BPM) and heart rate (40-120 BPM); **17-keypoint pose** from WiFi; **fall detection under 200ms**.
- Through-wall sensing up to ~5m. Runs at the edge on ESP32. Integrates with Home Assistant, Matter, Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa.
- **105 edge modules** across health, security, building, retail, industrial.
